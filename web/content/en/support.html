Start with the guide and the troubleshooting page. If that does not solve it, send a request from the support site and we answer by email and in the app.

Support site: support.enterworlds.space. Search the help articles, send a request (a problem, your account, account recovery, deleting your account and data, a report, a security issue, a suggestion) and follow your requests. You can also write to support@enterworlds.space.

Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Bug reports and feature requests all go to GitHub Issues. You need a GitHub account to open one.

  1. Search first

    Search the open issues. If you find the same problem, add what you know in a comment instead of opening a new issue.

  2. Open a new issue

    Click New issue and write a short title that describes the problem or the feature. Write in Arabic or English.

  3. Follow the replies

    We answer in the issue itself, and may ask you for more details or to try a newer version.

Issues on GitHub are public. Do not put your email or any personal details in them, and do not paste the content of your private pages.

What to include in a report

Version
The Worlds version number, as in the name of the file you downloaded or on its release page on GitHub.
Device
Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android or the browser, with the system version or the browser's name.
Steps
What you did, in order, until the problem appeared, what you expected, and what happened instead.
Screenshot or recording
A screenshot or a short recording, with the error message text exactly as it appeared.

Never include API keys, sign-in codes, passwords or any other secret, and keep them out of screenshots. Cover your email and other people's names if they show.

Security issues

If you find a security problem in the apps or the services, do not open a public issue. Send it privately through a private security advisory on GitHub, which only the people who run the repository can see. Explain the problem and how to reproduce it, and do not test it on other people's accounts or data.

Source code

The Worlds source code is published at github.com/justpainful/Worlds to read and contribute to; it is not open source. What is allowed is in the terms of use and the license text. Every build is on the releases page.

Common questions

Is Worlds free?

Yes. Downloading it, using it and creating an account are free today, and there is no paid plan in the app. If you use an AI provider with your own key, that provider bills you for what you use there.

Does it work offline?

The apps work offline. You only need the internet to share and sync, and for AI providers on the internet. Local models such as Ollama work offline. The web version needs a connection to open.

Where are my pages?

In the apps they are saved on your device in one workspace file. On Windows it is %APPDATA%\Worlds\worlds.db. In the web version they are saved in your browser, and on your account too if you sign in. Pages you share are also kept on the sync service. The details are in the privacy policy.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. An account is optional. You only need one to share a page with someone, sync between your devices, and keep the web copy on your account.

Which AI do I need?

You do not need any to use Worlds. If you want one, pick one of three kinds in the app: Claude Code or Codex with your own sign-in, an API key from a provider such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini, or a local model on your device such as Ollama or LM Studio. The assistant in the web version is a demo today and does not contact any provider.

Is my data used to train AI?

Worlds does not train any model, and AI requests never reach us: they go from your device straight to the provider you chose. Whether that provider trains its models on your requests is set by its terms and your account settings there, so check them with the provider. With a local model, nothing leaves your device.
