Settings

This page walks through the Settings sections of the Windows app in the order they appear, with the main options in each.

Opening Settings

Click the settings button next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar, or type "Settings" in the command palette. At the top of the section list is a Search field that finds a setting by a word in it, then the Profile, banner and blocks card that opens your profile.

In the Arabic interface product names stay as they are: the Claude and Discord sections keep those names.

Account

Signed out: "Without an account Worlds works entirely on this PC. With one you can join Team workspaces and share pages." Once you sign in, the section holds:

  • Workspaces: your Personal workspace and your Team workspaces, a People button for each, and Join a workspace with an invite link.
  • Passkeys: add one to sign in with Windows Hello.
  • Devices: every computer signed in to your account, and removing one.
  • Server: the server your account connects to.
  • Sign out of Worlds on this PC.

See Your Worlds account and Sharing.

Appearance

  • Theme: dark. A light appearance is planned.
  • Accent: used sparingly, for selection, mentions and focus.
  • Language: Automatic follows Windows, or English, or العربية. Pages always keep their own text direction.
  • Window, Transparency: Off, Subtle (Mica, tints the window with your wallpaper) or Glass (Acrylic, blurs whatever is behind it).
  • Glass: Glass quality (Full, Reduced or Solid), Glass tone (Automatic, Light or Dark), Glass density, Adapt to performance, which steps the glass down while frames are slow, and Liquid Glass background behind the profile and Home.
  • Motion: System or Reduced. Density: Comfortable or Compact.

Editor

  • Paragraph direction: Direction follows the text (each paragraph follows its own first letter, so mixed Arabic and English stay intact), Right to left, or Left to right.
  • Spell check: on or off.

Packs

The Study pack switch. Turning a pack off hides its views and items without deleting anything; turning it back on brings them all back. See Packs.

Claude

  • Status: whether Claude Code is ready on this PC.
  • Stay signed in: one sign-in that lasts about a year.
  • Model and effort: Spark, Orbit or Nova, with Glance, Focus or Deep.
  • Use Worlds from Claude Code sessions: the Register button adds the Worlds tool server to Claude Code.
  • Global instructions: instructions Claude reads in every chat.

See Claude and other AI providers and MCP server.

Providers

The default provider and model, then four groups: Sign in (Claude Code and Codex), API keys, Running on this PC and Local model servers. Details in Ways to connect the assistant.

Automations

The Background group: automations run inside Worlds on this PC, and if the PC is off, runs are missed and recorded as skipped.

  • Keep running in the tray: closing the window keeps Worlds in the notification area so schedules still run.
  • Start with Windows: starts hidden in the tray so schedules survive a restart.

See Automations.

Integrations

Opens the Integrations view.

Discord

The bridge card, with fields for the bot machine's address, SSH user, Module port and Key file on host. See Discord bridge.

Storage

  • Data folder: everything lives in one folder on this PC, the database and your attachments. Show in Explorer opens it.
  • Backups: Worlds copies the database once a day, before every update to its storage format, and whenever you ask. Back up now makes a copy, and Restore next to any backup takes effect the next time Worlds starts. The Backups folder is listed too.
  • Sample workspace: a first year student's week in your interface language, made on this device without a connection: pages, a document, a spreadsheet, a drawing, slides, two subjects with lessons and an exam, tasks and events. Load adds it and Remove takes it away.

Advanced

  • Developer tools: adds the Material Lab to the command palette for tuning glass.
  • Performance overlay: frame time, memory and startup time in a corner of the window.
  • Reset layout: closes all tabs and panes and returns to Home.

In the other apps

The Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac apps have their own, shorter settings screens, with for example the account and AI providers. The sections above are for the Windows app.
