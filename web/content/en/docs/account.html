Your Worlds account

You do not need an account to use Worlds: without one the app works fully on your computer. An account is for two things: working with other people in Team workspaces, and keeping your web workspace on your account so you can open it from any browser.

What the account is for

  • In the apps: "An account lets you join Team workspaces and work on pages with other people. Your Personal pages stay on this PC."
  • On the web: your pages stay in the browser and work without a connection. Sign in to keep them on your account and open them anywhere.

The same account works in the Worlds app on your computer and phone and on the web. How data moves is explained in Sync.

Creating an account

There is no separate sign-up step: the account is created the first time you enter a correct email code.

  1. Start

    On Windows: on the first screen click Sign In or Create Account, or open Settings, Account. On the web: open go.enterworlds.space.

  2. Enter your name and email

    Under Create your account: "Your name is what people see when you share pages. Your email is for sign-in codes and recovery." The name can be up to 80 characters. A username is picked for you, and you can change it later in your profile.

  3. Enter the code

    A 6-digit code arrives. Type it under Enter the code and click Continue. You see "Your account is ready".

  4. A passkey (Windows)

    In the Windows app, Worlds then offers Add a Passkey, or Not Now.

Signing in with an email code

Every email sign-in works the same way: type your email, click Send Code, then type the code you receive.

Sender
Worlds, from no-reply@enterworlds.space
Subject
The code followed by "is your Worlds sign-in code", or in Arabic if the app is in Arabic
Code
6 digits, works for 10 minutes and only once
Wrong tries
After 5 wrong tries the code stops working; ask for a new one
Codes
At most 5 codes per email address per hour

If the code does not arrive, look in your spam folder, then click Send a New Code. If you go over a limit you see "Too many attempts", so wait a while and try again.

On Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac the account screen has the same steps: Send code or Send verification code, then Verify.

Passkeys

A passkey signs you in without an email code. In the Windows app: "A passkey signs you in with Windows Hello (face, fingerprint or PIN) or your phone. Without one, sign in with an emailed code."

  • Add one in Settings, Account, Passkeys.
  • To sign in, click Sign In with a Passkey. Your browser opens to confirm it is you, then brings you back to Worlds. To use the code instead, click Use an Email Code Instead.

Passkeys work in the Windows app. On the web, sign in with the email code. On Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac, sign-in uses the email code for now.

Devices

Settings, Account, Devices lists every computer signed in to your account. Removing one signs it out at once, including any open shared pages.

Signing out

Signing out does not delete your pages from the device:

  • Windows: in Settings, Account, choose Sign out of Worlds on this PC. Every page stays on the PC, and Team pages are hidden until you sign in again.
  • Android: the app asks to sign the device out from the server, and local pages stay on the device.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: Sign out. Saved copies of shared pages that belong to another account are removed the next time the shared pages list loads.
  • Web: ends the account session in this browser.

Where accounts live

The account service is at login.enterworlds.space and runs on Cloudflare. It sends the codes and keeps passkeys, workspaces, invites and permissions. See Privacy for what is kept.
