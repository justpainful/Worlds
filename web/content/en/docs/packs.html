Packs

A pack is an optional set of features you turn on for what you use Worlds for: new kinds of items, sidebar entries, Home sections and commands. If you do not turn a pack on, you see nothing of it.

How packs work

  • A pack that is off shows nothing anywhere.
  • Turning a pack off hides its views and items without deleting anything. Turning it back on brings them all back.
  • Turn packs on and off in Settings, Packs.

The Study pack

Study is the first pack, and the one most people start with. Its description: "Subjects, a class timetable, assignments, exams and lessons, for school or university."

Turning it on

  1. From Home

    Worlds asks you once: "Do you use Worlds for school or university?" Click Turn on Study to turn the pack on and open it, or No, keep it simple.

  2. Or from Settings

    Open Settings, Packs and switch on Study pack. You can change this at any time.

Once it is on, Study appears in the sidebar and the command palette.

What it adds

  • Subjects with teachers, rooms and colours.
  • A weekly class timetable in Calendar.
  • Assignments, exams and things To bring or print.
  • Lesson notes filed under each subject.
  • A study summary on Home.
  • A Study view with Today's classes, Due in the next two weeks, Subjects, Timetable, Assignments, Exams and Lessons.

Items and their fields

ItemFields
SubjectTeacher, room, code, colour, term, books (with page count and current page), and links
ClassClass time, days of the week, an end date, and dates to skip for holidays or cancelled classes
LessonUnit, number and the date it was taught
AssignmentDue date and time, the date it was given, teacher, points and grade, instructions, and whether it was submitted
ExamDate and time, what it covers, location, points and score
To bring or printWhen it is needed, whether it is ready, and Needs printing with copies, colour mode, Double-sided and pages range

Ordinary tasks and events show in Calendar alongside classes and exams.

Study and Claude

Ask Claude in a chat to organise your study pages. In a coming release, Claude and any program connected through the MCP server get planning tools that read, create and update study items: a subject with its weekly classes in one request, an assignment with its due date, or an exam with what it covers. Each of these changes saves a version first and can be undone.

All packs

Worlds has 39 packs today, in seven families: education and training, work and business, field work, services and hospitality, creative, tech and media, community, and individuals. Each adds its own kinds of items and fields, tables and boards, a Home section with figures and alerts, ready requests for Claude, and examples in both languages. They come with a Daily summary in Inbox option that collects once a day what needs your attention, and pack roles in Team workspaces.

Every pack has a page with what it adds in detail: all packs.
