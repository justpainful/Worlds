The story

It started with too many tools

The project's owner kept moving between tools to organise their notes, so they built Campus for studying: notes taken on the phone, then synced to the computer locally. That version stayed limited to study and lacked things they needed outside it, so they rebuilt it from the ground up as Worlds.

  1. 01Notes on the phoneCampus began with a clear need: studying, and moving notes locally.
  2. 02Needs that grewThe work came to need files, drawing, slides and connected projects.
  3. 03A full rebuildThe structure and interface changed, and the idea widened until it became Worlds.

Claude inside Worlds

It reads and edits your pages with Worlds tools, and every edit can be undone

On Windows you choose Claude Code or Codex, an API key from a provider you pick, or a model running offline on your own computer such as Ollama. On Android it edits pages and adds tasks, with one-step undo, and on iPhone, iPad and Mac it is a real conversation with the same providers. Requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose.

The owner's role

How was Worlds built?

This is the story of using AI tools to build the app, and it is separate from the AI features inside it. The result of each review comes back as direction for the next step.

  1. The ownerDefines the problemScattered tools, and the limits of the Campus study version.
  2. The ownerChooses the directionRebuild the project, its structure and its design instead of keeping it limited to study.
  3. Claude and CodexDevelopment tools they directHelp write and change the code and try solutions in the chosen direction.
  4. The ownerReviews and decidesFollows the result and decides what needs changing or finishing.
