Apps and platforms

Worlds runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android, and in the browser. The Windows and Linux apps are the most complete; the Apple and Android apps are built for their own systems and catch up feature by feature. This page lists what really works in version 1.1.0.

Every file is on the GitHub releases page. Before installing, you can check that a file is intact with its fingerprint in SHA256SUMS.txt; the install page shows how.

  • Every kind of resource: pages, documents, tables, drawings, presentations, galleries, files and projects.
  • A complete Arabic interface, right to left, with dates and lists.
  • Claude edits pages with Worlds tools through Claude Code or Codex, an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI or OpenRouter, or a model on your computer such as Ollama or LM Studio that works offline. Every edit can be undone.
  • Automations, the Discord bridge that waits for your approval before each send, and the MCP server for connecting Claude Code to your workspace.

What works in the three Apple apps (Mac, iPhone and iPad):

  • Editing, creating and moving to Trash pages, documents, tables, drawings and presentations in your own workspace when its database is on a local disk, with a version check before every save. In iCloud or a cloud folder they stay read only, with a note that says why.
  • Drawing: the eraser, masks, effects, nested layers, every kind of brush and shape, and correction of hand-drawn shapes, in sRGB colours that match Windows.
  • Tables with values calculated by the same formula engine as Windows and Android.
  • A real AI conversation with the same providers, with the key kept in the Keychain. It does not edit pages yet.

Android 8 or later, and Chromebooks.

Step by step, with pictures. The file is signed with a development key and is not from Google Play.
  • The AI reads, creates and edits pages and adds tasks with the same tools as Windows, and all the changes of one reply can be undone in one step. The key is kept in the Keystore.
  • Study and planning: screens for tasks, goals, the inbox, the calendar (a list and a week view) and subjects with lessons, homework and exams, and a quick add that understands "tomorrow 4pm" and Arabic such as «بكرة».
  • Automations: an AI summary through the provider you chose, and a notification on the device next to Discord.

Browser

Address
app.enterworlds.space
Demo
demo.enterworlds.space, with a ready workspace to try things in; it keeps nothing on an account.
Guide
Worlds in the browser
  • Nothing to install. Your pages are kept in your browser and are still there after you close the tab.
  • Create an account and sign in, and your workspace is also kept on your account, so it opens in any browser you sign in to.
  • Claude and the device tools (such as automations and Discord) work in the apps, not in the browser.
