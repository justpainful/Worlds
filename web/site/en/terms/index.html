In a shared workspace, members can view, copy or edit pages according to the permissions the owner and admins set. Think about that before you share. You are responsible for having the right to share what you share.

Your account

  • An account is optional. You only need one to share pages, sync between your devices, and keep the web copy on your account.
  • Use an email address you own and can reach, because sign-in codes go to it.
  • A username is 3 to 20 characters: lowercase Latin letters, digits, "_" and ".", and some names are reserved. Do not pick a name that impersonates someone.
  • Keep your email, devices and passkeys safe. Never give your sign-in code to anyone; we will never ask you for it.
  • You are responsible for what happens with your account, including what the AI or an automation does on your behalf.
  • If you think someone got into your account, sign out the devices you do not recognise in account settings, and tell us as described in Contact.

  • Publish or store content that is illegal, or that infringes other people's rights such as copyright and privacy.

  • Spread malware or scam links.
  • Try to get into an account or page you have no access to, or get around permissions or request limits.
  • Overload the services on purpose, create accounts in bulk by automation, or send sign-in codes to other people's email addresses.
  • Use the service as a public place to host files or hand them out to people outside your workspace.

Service limits

  • A file attached to a shared page can be up to 5 GB.
  • The web copy on your account can be up to 32 MB, and each file attached to it up to 25 MB.

We may change these limits according to what the services can carry.
