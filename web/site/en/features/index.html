Worlds features

Worlds is one workspace to write, plan, calculate, draw and present, saved on your device first. It was designed in Arabic, right to left, from the start, and runs for free on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android and in the browser. This page gathers its features in version 1.0.

Pages and writing

  • Block pages: headings, checklists, tables, callouts, columns, toggles and links between pages.
  • Advanced blocks: synced blocks that appear on several pages, buttons, transcribed voice notes, footnotes, forms whose responses become pages, JavaScript and Python that run in the page, a whiteboard and maps of places.
  • Paper-sized documents in A4 and Letter, with print and PDF.
  • LaTeX equations, Mermaid diagrams and charts from tables.
  • Arabic spelling for hamza, taa marbuta and alef maqsura, read aloud, side-by-side translation and a time machine for page versions.

Pages guide and documents guide

Tasks, planning and study

  • Tasks with steps, priorities and repeats, plus events, a calendar, goals and an inbox for quick capture.
  • Quick add that understands Arabic as people type it, such as «بكرة 4م», as well as English like «tomorrow 4pm».
  • The Hijri date beside every day, and a Friday and Saturday weekend.
  • Student tools: subjects, lessons, assignments, exams, things to bring and a focus timer.
  • 39 packs for study, work, community and life, including Teacher, Freelance, Small business and Writing.

Packs guide

Cognition: tools for thinking and memory

  • Your world: your pages are islands, links are bridges, open questions are fog and moments of understanding are stars.
  • Spaced-repetition cards that come back before you forget them, with a daily review.
  • Open questions, sparks that connect two ideas, Jigsaw boards that reveal the missing pieces of a research or a decision, and the Aha! journal.
  • Thinking blocks: claim and evidence, weighted pros and cons, a decision log, five whys and the page fingerprint.

Cognition in the changelog

Spreadsheets

Multi-sheet spreadsheets with formulas, sorting, filters, conditional formatting and frozen rows. They open and save XLSX and CSV files, and count numbers typed on an Arabic keyboard.

Spreadsheets guide

Drawing and presentations

  • A drawing workspace with brushes, layers, masks and shapes with shape correction, Arabic text, mind maps and drawing on the screen.
  • Presentations with speaker notes, and turning a page into slides.

Drawing guide and presentations guide

Claude and AI

Claude reads and edits your pages, and every change can be undone. Select text and ask to improve, shorten or translate it, or ask your space and get an answer from your own pages with links to its sources. Use Claude Code, Codex, an API key from several providers, or a local model that works offline.

Claude guide

Files and publishing

  • Import from Notion, Obsidian, Word, Markdown, HTML and CSV, and export to Word, PDF and EPUB.
  • Text read from pictures and PDFs, a browser extension, and a reading list that works offline.
  • Publishing to the web, temporary links with a password, a private email address whose messages become pages, and Google and Apple calendars.

Media and files guide and sharing guide

Privacy and sync

Your pages are saved on your device, and accounts and sync are optional. Turn them on to sync between your devices and work live with your team, with a version history for every page. Private pages can be locked with a code or Windows Hello.
