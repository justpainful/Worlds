The story of Worlds

Worlds started on 4 October 2026 as one place for pages and ideas. Seven weeks later it runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, and version 1.0 adds more than fifty new features. Here is the story, in order.

Version 0.1: the start

4 October 2026

  • Pages made of blocks: text, headings, lists, tables, pictures, video, files and embeds.
  • Liquid Glass: real glass that bends the light behind bars and menus.
  • Claude reads and writes your pages, and every change can be undone.
  • Automations, a Discord bridge that waits for your approval, and daily backups.

Version 0.2: every platform

10 November 2026

  • Native apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and ChromeOS.
  • Student tools: subjects, lessons, assignments, exams and things to bring.
  • Planning: tasks, events, calendar, goals, an inbox and a focus timer.
  • Spreadsheets with formulas, and a full drawing space with brushes, layers, masks and frames.

Version 0.3: AI and Arabic

12 November 2026

  • AI on every platform: Claude Code or Codex, any provider with an API key, or a model on your own computer.
  • A complete right-to-left Arabic interface, and search that forgives alif and taa marbuta spellings.
  • A sample workspace, a web version, a tour, HTML pages and sector packs.

Version 0.3.2: drawing and public profiles

19 November 2026

  • Drawing over the screen with nine brushes, and drawing tools in Worlds' own menus.
  • A public profile with your picture, banner and blocks on profile.enterworlds.space.
  • Concept maps on every platform.

Version 1.0: the biggest update

November 2026

  • Pages: synced blocks, buttons, voice notes, footnotes, forms, runnable code, a whiteboard, a map of places, calendar and timeline views, relations, formulas and reminders.
  • Claude and writing: Claude in the text shows the change before you accept it; ask your space, comments, read aloud, side-by-side translation, a time machine, Arabic spelling and a weekly summary.
  • Cognition: your world as islands, bridges and stars, memory cards, open questions, sparks between ideas, Jigsaw boards and thinking blocks.
  • The app: a light theme, a Home of widgets or one you build yourself, the links map, private pages, plugins and themes, and shortcuts of your own.
  • Files: import from Notion, Obsidian and Word; export to Word, PDF, EPUB and your whole space; a reading list, a browser extension, and the text in pictures and PDFs.
  • Sharing: publish to the web, share links with a password and an end date, email to Worlds, Google and Apple calendars, a local API and webhooks, Siri and an Android widget, and a Linux build.

Every file is on the GitHub releases page; what runs where is on Apps.
