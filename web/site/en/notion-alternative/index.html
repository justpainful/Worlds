Worlds for people looking for a Notion alternative

If you use Notion and want a workspace where Arabic feels at home and your pages stay on your device, this page explains what Worlds offers and how to bring your pages over.

Why Worlds

  • Arabic from the start: a fully right-to-left interface, the Hijri date, Arabic spelling, search that finds a word however its alef and taa marbuta were typed, and shortcuts that work on an Arabic keyboard.
  • Your pages on your device: saved on your device first and working offline, with accounts and sync optional.
  • Free: on Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone, iPad, Android and in the browser, with the source published on GitHub.
  • More than pages: spreadsheets with formulas, a drawing workspace, presentations, tasks and a calendar, and Cognition for memory and understanding.
  • Claude in your pages: it reads and edits, every change can be undone, and you choose the provider or run a local model.

What will feel familiar

Writing in blocks, the / menu to add any block, nested pages, templates, collections in table, calendar and timeline views, relations and formula properties, synced blocks, and sharing with your team.

Moving your pages from Notion

  1. Export your workspace from Notion

    In Notion's settings, export the workspace as Markdown and CSV. You get a zip file.

  2. Import it into Worlds

    In Worlds, open the command palette with Ctrl + K, choose Import Pages and pick the zip file. Each Notion page becomes a Worlds page with its name, and Notion's tables become tables inside the pages.

  3. Carry on where you left off

    Your pages are now on your device. Turn on sync if you want them on your other devices.
