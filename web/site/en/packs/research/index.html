Packs / Education and training

The Research pack

References and citations, reading notes, thesis chapters, an experiment log and supervisor meetings.

What the pack adds

  • A library of references with their citation
  • Reading notes on each source
  • Thesis chapters by stage
  • An experiment log
  • Meetings with your supervisor and what to do next

What you keep in it

References

A source you read or cite. Fields: Authors, Year, Type, Journal or publisher, Reading.

Thesis chapters

A chapter of your thesis or paper. Fields: Order, Status, Words, Due.

Experiment log

One run, what you did and what came out. Fields: Date, Question, Result.

Supervisor meetings

A meeting, what was agreed and the next steps. Fields: Date, Next step, Next step done.

Tables and boards

  • Library (Table)
  • Reading list (Board)
  • Chapters (Board)
  • Experiment log (Table)

On Home

Figures: References, Still to read, Thesis words.

Alerts when something needs you: Next steps from meetings.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Format my references
  • Find gaps in my literature
  • Prepare for my supervisor meeting

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Researcher: sees every item, and edits
  • Supervisor: sees every item, read only
  • Colleague: sees every item, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Research. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
