Packs / Field work

The Safety and inspection pack

Checklists, site visits, incidents and corrective actions until each is closed.

What the pack adds

  • Inspection visits with a score
  • Incidents and near misses by severity
  • Corrective actions with an owner and a date
  • Reusable checklists

What you keep in it

Inspections

A visit to check a site against a checklist. Fields: Site, Date, Inspected by, Checklist, Score %.

Incidents

Something that went wrong or nearly did. Fields: Site, Date, Severity, Status.

Corrective actions

A fix with an owner and a date. Fields: From incident, From inspection, Owner, Due, Done.

Checklists

A list of checks used on every visit. Fields: Topic.

Tables and boards

  • Corrective actions (Table)
  • Incidents (Board)
  • Inspections (Table)
  • Checklists (Table)

On Home

Figures: Open actions, Incidents this month, Average inspection score.

Alerts when something needs you: Overdue corrective actions, Serious incidents open.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write an incident report
  • Build a checklist

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Safety officer: sees every item, and edits
  • Site supervisor: sees every item, and edits
  • Management: sees every item, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Safety and inspection. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
