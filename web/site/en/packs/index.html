Packs

A pack is an optional set of features you turn on for what you use Worlds for: kinds of items with their fields, tables and boards, figures and alerts on Home, and ready requests for Claude. Every pack is free, works in Arabic and English, and you can turn on several at once.

Education and training

  • StudySubjects, a class timetable, assignments, exams and lessons, for school or university.
  • TeacherClasses, students, lesson plans, attendance and a gradebook, for school teachers and lecturers.
  • Quran circlesCircles and students, memorisation and revision by surah and ayah, and attendance.
  • Training centresCourses, trainees, sessions and certificates, for training centres and trainers.
  • ResearchReferences and citations, reading notes, thesis chapters, an experiment log and supervisor meetings.
  • Admission and scholarshipsUniversities and scholarships, their requirements, documents and deadlines.

Work and business

  • FreelanceClients, quotes, invoices with VAT, payments and hours, for people who work for themselves.
  • Small businessProducts and stock, orders by stage, customers, suppliers and expenses, for shops and home businesses.
  • SalesDeals by stage, follow ups on time, a call log and monthly targets, for sales people and teams.
  • RecruitingOpen roles, candidates by stage, interviews and scorecards, for hiring managers and HR.
  • Real estate brokerageListings, clients, viewings and contracts, for real estate brokers and offices.
  • Restaurants and cafesThe menu with costs, stock of ingredients, staff shifts and suppliers.
  • Consulting and coachingClients, sessions, plans and goals and session notes, for consultants and coaches. Not for medical care.
  • Administrative correspondenceIncoming and outgoing letters with reference numbers, minutes of meetings and circulars.

Field work

  • Contracting and maintenanceSites, work orders, technicians, equipment, daily reports and bills of quantities.
  • Transport and deliveryTrips, drivers, vehicles with their servicing and papers, and shipments by status.
  • Farming and livestockFields and crops by season, irrigation, and livestock with vaccinations and births.
  • Safety and inspectionChecklists, site visits, incidents and corrective actions until each is closed.

Services and hospitality

  • Hotels and short rentalsUnits, bookings, guests and a cleaning rota, for hotels, chalets and short rentals.
  • SalonsAppointments, services and prices, staff and regular clients, for salons and barbers.
  • Property managementUnits, tenants, leases and rent payments, and maintenance requests, for owners and managers.
  • Hajj, Umrah and travel companiesGroups, travellers and their documents, housing, buses and the programme day by day.

Creative, tech and media

  • Writing and worldbuildingCharacters, places, chapters and scenes, events and lore, for novelists, screenwriters and game makers.
  • Dev teamsBugs and tasks in sprints, releases, design decisions and API notes, for software teams.
  • Content and marketingA publishing calendar, an idea bank, campaigns, drafts per platform and their results.
  • Photo and videoShoots, clients, deliveries and gear, for photographers and video makers.
  • DesignProjects, references and mood boards, rounds of feedback and deliveries to the client.
  • Podcast and mediaEpisodes from idea to published, guests, outlines and scripts, and the publishing schedule.
  • Game makingA design document, levels, art and sound assets, balance tables and bugs.

Community

  • Nonprofits and volunteeringMembers and their subscriptions, volunteers and their hours, and activities.
  • Sports clubsPlayers, training sessions with attendance, matches and results, for clubs and academies.
  • EventsGuests and invitations, the programme, the budget and suppliers, for weddings, conferences and gatherings.

Individuals

  • Personal financeA monthly budget, expenses, subscriptions and savings goals.
  • Health and fitnessHabits, workouts, meals, and reminders for medicine and doctor visits. For your own use, not medical advice.
  • Home and familyChores, the shopping list, a meal plan and family dates.
  • TravelTrips, bookings, documents and packing lists.
  • Job seekerApplications by stage, interviews and the versions of your CV.
  • ReadingBooks, quotes, reading goals and notes per book.
  • My carMaintenance, running costs, and registration and insurance renewals.
