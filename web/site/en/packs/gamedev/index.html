Packs / Creative, tech and media

The Game making pack

A design document, levels, art and sound assets, balance tables and bugs.

What the pack adds

  • Design document sections
  • Levels by stage, with their goal and difficulty
  • Art and sound assets and who makes them
  • Balance tables for units and items
  • Bugs found in play tests

What you keep in it

Design document

One part of the game design document. Fields: Topic, Status.

Levels

A level, its goal and how hard it is. Fields: Order, Goal, Difficulty, Status, Play time, minutes.

Assets

Art, sound or music the game needs. Fields: Type, Level, Artist, Status.

Balance table

Numbers for one unit, weapon or item. Fields: Health, Damage, Speed, Cost.

Bugs

Something broken found in a play test. Fields: Level, Severity, Fixed.

Tables and boards

  • Levels (Board)
  • Assets (Board)
  • Design document (Table)
  • Balance table (Table)
  • Bugs (Table)

On Home

Figures: Levels done, Assets needed, Open bugs.

Alerts when something needs you: Crashes to fix.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Check my balance table
  • Design a level

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Game director: sees every item, and edits
  • Developer or artist: sees every item, and edits
  • Play tester: sees every item, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Game making. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
