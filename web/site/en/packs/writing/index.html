The Writing and worldbuilding pack

Characters, places, chapters and scenes, events and lore, for novelists, screenwriters and game makers.

What the pack adds

  • Characters, places, groups, objects and the systems of your world
  • Chapters and scenes on a board from idea to final
  • A timeline of events in story order
  • A daily word goal
  • Claude checks consistency, summarises chapters and suggests names

What you keep in it

Characters

Someone in the story. Fields: Role, Age, Lives in, Belongs to, Wants.

Places

Somewhere the story happens. Fields: Type, Inside.

Chapters

A part of the book. Fields: Order, Status, Words.

Scenes

One scene in a chapter. Fields: Chapter, Point of view, Place, Status, Words.

Timeline

Something that happens in the world's history. Fields: Order in time, When in the story, Place, Who.

Societies

A family, faction, guild or nation. Fields: Type, Based in, Goal.

Systems

How magic, technology or society works. Fields: Type, Cost or limit.

Objects

A thing that matters to the story. Fields: Owner, Kept at, What it does.

Word count

Words written on a day. Fields: Date, Words, Goal, Chapter.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Check consistency
  • Summarise a chapter
  • Suggest names

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Writer: sees every item, and edits
  • Co-writer: sees every item, and edits
  • Editor: sees every item, read only
  • Beta reader: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Writing and worldbuilding. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
