Packs / Community

The Events pack

Guests and invitations, the programme, the budget and suppliers, for weddings, conferences and gatherings.

What the pack adds

  • Guests with their invitation and reply
  • The programme hour by hour
  • A budget against what was spent
  • Suppliers and what is booked with each

What you keep in it

Events

A gathering you organise. Fields: Date, Venue, Expected guests, Budget.

Guests

Someone invited. Fields: Occasion, Phone, Party size, RSVP, Side.

Programme

What happens at a time. Fields: Occasion, Time, Who, Where.

Budget

What a part of the event costs. Fields: Occasion, Supplier, Planned, Actual, Paid.

Suppliers

Catering, venue, photography, flowers. Fields: Service, Phone, Booked.

Tables and boards

  • Guests (Board)
  • Programme (Table)
  • Budget (Table)
  • Suppliers (Table)
  • Events (Table)

On Home

Figures: Coming, Spent, Planned budget.

Alerts when something needs you: Guests not invited yet, Suppliers not booked.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write the invitation
  • Build a programme

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Organiser: sees every item, and edits
  • Helper: sees every item, and edits
  • Host: sees every item, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Events. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
