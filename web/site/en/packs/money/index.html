The Personal finance pack

A monthly budget, expenses, subscriptions and savings goals.

What the pack adds

  • Expenses by category against a monthly budget
  • Subscriptions and when they renew
  • Savings goals and how close you are
  • Claude explains where the money went

What you keep in it

Expenses

Money you spent. Fields: Amount, Date, Category, Paid with.

Budget

What you plan to spend on something each month. Fields: Monthly limit, Category.

Subscriptions

Something you pay for again and again. Fields: Price, Every, Renews on, Keep.

Savings goals

Money you are putting aside for something. Fields: Target, Saved, By.

On Home

Figures: Spent this month, Monthly budget, Saved so far.

Alerts when something needs you: Subscriptions renewing this week.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Explain my spending
  • Plan a savings goal

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Me: sees every item, and edits
  • Family member: sees every item, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Personal finance. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
