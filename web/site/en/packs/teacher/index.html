Classes, students, lesson plans, attendance and a gradebook, for school teachers and lecturers.

What it keeps 7

  • ClassesA group you teach, with its subject and room: Subject, Grade or level, Room
  • StudentsOne student, their class and who to contact: Class, Number, Parent or guardian, Guardian's phone
  • TimetableA weekly period in your timetable: Class, Day, Starts, Ends, Room
  • Lesson plansWhat a lesson covers and how it runs: Class, Date, Unit, Status
  • AssessmentsA quiz, test, project or homework that gets a mark: Class, Type, Date, Out of, Marking
  • GradebookOne student's mark in one assessment: Student, Assessment, Score, Comment
  • AttendanceWhether a student was in class on a day: Student, Date, Status, Parent told, Note
