Packs / Education and training

The Teacher pack

Classes, students, lesson plans, attendance and a gradebook, for school teachers and lecturers.

What the pack adds

  • Classes and their students, with a parent to call
  • Attendance in one tap per student
  • Lesson plans and a weekly timetable
  • Assessments, a gradebook with averages and work waiting to be marked
  • Claude writes lesson plans, questions and notes to parents

What you keep in it

Classes

A group you teach, with its subject and room. Fields: Subject, Grade or level, Room.

Students

One student, their class and who to contact. Fields: Class, Number, Parent or guardian, Guardian's phone.

Timetable

A weekly period in your timetable. Fields: Class, Day, Starts, Ends, Room.

Lesson plans

What a lesson covers and how it runs. Fields: Class, Date, Unit, Status.

Assessments

A quiz, test, project or homework that gets a mark. Fields: Class, Type, Date, Out of, Marking.

Gradebook

One student's mark in one assessment. Fields: Student, Assessment, Score, Comment.

Attendance

Whether a student was in class on a day. Fields: Student, Date, Status, Parent told, Note.

Ready messages: Tell the parent.

On Home

Figures: Students, Absent today, Waiting to be marked, Average grade.

Alerts when something needs you: Absences to tell parents about, Assessments to mark.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write a lesson plan
  • Draft questions from a lesson
  • Summarise a class's results
  • Write to a parent

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Teacher: sees every item, and edits
  • Assistant teacher: sees every item, and edits
  • Student: sees only what is assigned to them, read only
  • Parent: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Send from here, and each item arrives in the member's own Worlds: Assessments to every Student.

Worlds fills in the message from the item and opens it in WhatsApp or email, so you read it and send it yourself.

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Teacher. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
