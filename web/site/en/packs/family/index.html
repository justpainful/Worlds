Packs / Individuals

The Home and family pack

Chores, the shopping list, a meal plan and family dates.

What the pack adds

  • Chores shared between the family
  • A shopping list everyone adds to
  • A meal plan for the week
  • Birthdays, visits and family dates

What you keep in it

Chores

A job around the house. Fields: Who, Day, Done.

Shopping list

Something to buy. Fields: Quantity, Section, Bought.

Meal plan

What is cooking on a day. Fields: Day, Meal, Cook.

Family dates

A birthday, visit or occasion. Fields: Date, Type.

Tables and boards

  • Shopping list (Table)
  • Chores (Table)
  • Meal plan (Table)
  • Family dates (Table)

On Home

Figures: To buy, Chores left.

Alerts when something needs you: Coming up this week.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Plan a week of meals
  • Share out the chores

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Parent: sees every item, and edits
  • Child: sees only what is assigned to them, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Home and family. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
