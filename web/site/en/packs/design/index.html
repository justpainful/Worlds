Packs / Creative, tech and media

The Design pack

Projects, references and mood boards, rounds of feedback and deliveries to the client.

What the pack adds

  • Design projects by stage
  • References and mood boards with links
  • Each round of feedback and what changed
  • Files delivered to the client

What you keep in it

Design projects

A piece of design work for a client. Fields: Client, Due, Fee, Rounds included, Status.

References

An image or work that inspires the project. Fields: Project, Kind of reference, Why it fits.

Feedback

What the client asked to change. Fields: Project, Round number, Date, Done.

Tables and boards

  • Design projects (Board)
  • References (Table)
  • Feedback (Table)

On Home

Figures: Active projects, Fees in progress.

Alerts when something needs you: Feedback to act on, Due this week.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Turn a brief into questions
  • Present a design

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Designer: sees every item, and edits
  • Studio member: sees every item, and edits
  • Client: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Design. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
