Packs / Individuals

The Travel pack

Trips, bookings, documents and packing lists.

What the pack adds

  • Trips with dates and budget
  • Flights, hotels and cars booked for each
  • Passports and visas with their expiry
  • A packing list per trip

What you keep in it

Trips

Somewhere you are going. Fields: Going to, Departs, Returns, Budget, Status.

Bookings

A flight, hotel, car or ticket. Fields: Trip, Type, Date, Booking reference, Cost.

Documents

A passport, visa or licence. Fields: Whose, Type, Expires.

Packing list

Something to take. Fields: Trip, Packed.

Tables and boards

  • Trips (Board)
  • Bookings (Table)
  • Documents (Table)
  • Packing list (Table)

On Home

Figures: Trips booked, Booked so far.

Alerts when something needs you: Documents expiring within six months, Left to pack.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Plan my itinerary
  • Make a packing list

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Planner: sees every item, and edits
  • Traveller: sees every item, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Travel. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
