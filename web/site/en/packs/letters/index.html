Packs / Work and business

The Administrative correspondence pack

Incoming and outgoing letters with reference numbers, minutes of meetings and circulars.

What the pack adds

  • Incoming and outgoing letters numbered in order
  • Who a letter was referred to and its reply date
  • Minutes of meetings with decisions
  • Circulars and who has read them

What you keep in it

Incoming

A letter received, numbered and referred. Fields: Reference number, From, Received, Referred to, Reply by, Status.

Outgoing

A letter sent, numbered. Fields: Reference number, To, Sent, In reply to, Priority.

Minutes

What a meeting decided. Fields: Reference number, Date, Attendees, Follow up by.

Circulars

A notice to everyone. Fields: Reference number, Date, For, Read by everyone.

On Home

Figures: Letters to answer, Sent this month.

Alerts when something needs you: Replies overdue.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Draft a formal reply
  • Write minutes from notes

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Head of department: sees every item, and edits
  • Records clerk: sees every item, and edits
  • Staff: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Administrative correspondence. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
