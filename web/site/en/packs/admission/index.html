Packs / Education and training

The Admission and scholarships pack

Universities and scholarships, their requirements, documents and deadlines.

What the pack adds

  • Universities and scholarships you are applying to
  • Requirements and documents with their status
  • Deadlines on the calendar with warnings
  • Claude helps with personal statements

What you keep in it

Applications

A university or scholarship you apply to. Fields: University, Country, Degree, Deadline, Application fee, Status.

Documents

A paper an application needs. Fields: Application, Type, Expires, Ready.

Tables and boards

  • Applications (Board)
  • Deadlines (Table)
  • Documents (Table)

On Home

Figures: Applications, Accepted, Documents not ready.

Alerts when something needs you: Deadlines in the next two weeks, Documents about to expire.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Draft a personal statement
  • Ask for a recommendation letter

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Applicant: sees every item, and edits
  • Advisor: sees every item, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Admission and scholarships. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
