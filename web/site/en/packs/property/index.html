Packs / Services and hospitality

The Property management pack

Units, tenants, leases and rent payments, and maintenance requests, for owners and managers.

What the pack adds

  • Units and who lives in each
  • Leases with their end dates
  • Rent due and paid, with late payments flagged
  • Maintenance requests until fixed

What you keep in it

Units

An apartment, shop or office you let. Fields: Building, Type, Yearly rent, Status.

Tenants

Someone who rents a unit. Fields: Unit, Phone.

Leases

A rental contract and when it ends. Fields: Unit, Tenant, Starts, Ends, Yearly rent, Paid, Registered on Ejar.

Rent

A rent instalment and whether it was paid. Fields: Lease, Amount, Due, Paid.

Maintenance

Something to fix in a unit. Fields: Unit, Reported, Type, Cost, Status.

Tables and boards

  • Rent (Table)
  • Units (Table)
  • Leases (Table)
  • Maintenance (Board)
  • Tenants (Table)

On Home

Figures: Vacant units, Rent collected this month, Open requests.

Alerts when something needs you: Late rent, Leases ending within two months.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Remind a tenant about rent
  • Plan lease renewals

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Owner: sees every item, and edits
  • Property manager: sees every item, and edits
  • Tenant: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Property management. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
