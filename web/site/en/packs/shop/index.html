Packs / Work and business

The Small business pack

Products and stock, orders by stage, customers, suppliers and expenses, for shops and home businesses.

What the pack adds

  • Products with stock and a warning when it runs low
  • Orders moving from new to delivered
  • Customers and suppliers
  • Expenses and this month's takings
  • Claude writes product descriptions and answers customers

What you keep in it

Products

Something you sell, its price and how many are left. Fields: Code, Price, Cost, In stock, Reorder at, Supplier.

Orders

What a customer bought and where it is. Fields: Customer, Order number, Total, Date, Status, Channel.

Ready messages: Tell the customer.

Customers

Someone who buys from you. Fields: Phone, City.

Suppliers

Who you buy stock from. Fields: Contact person, Phone, Payment terms.

Stock movements

Stock coming in or going out. Fields: Product, Direction, Quantity, Date, Reason.

Expenses

Money the business spent. Fields: Amount, Date, Category, Supplier.

Tables and boards

  • Orders (Board)
  • Stock (Table)
  • Low stock (Table)
  • Customers (Table)
  • Suppliers (Table)
  • Expenses (Table)
  • Stock movements (Table)
  • Sales (Table)

On Home

Figures: Orders today, Takings this month, Expenses this month, Open orders.

Alerts when something needs you: Low stock, New orders waiting.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write a product description
  • Explain this month's sales
  • Answer a customer

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Owner: sees every item, and edits
  • Manager: sees every item, and edits
  • Staff: sees every item, and edits
  • Accountant: sees every item, read only

Worlds fills in the message from the item and opens it in WhatsApp or email, so you read it and send it yourself.

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Small business. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
