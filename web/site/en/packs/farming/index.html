Packs / Field work

The Farming and livestock pack

Fields and crops by season, irrigation, and livestock with vaccinations and births.

What the pack adds

  • Fields with what is planted and when to harvest
  • An irrigation log
  • Animals with vaccinations and births
  • Reminders before a vaccination is due

What you keep in it

Fields

A plot of land and what grows on it. Fields: Area, dunam, Crop, Season, Planted, Harvest, Status.

Irrigation log

When a field was watered and how much. Fields: Field, Date, Hours, Water, m³.

Livestock

One animal or a herd. Fields: Type, Tag, Head count, Next vaccination, Born.

Health records

A vaccination, treatment or birth. Fields: Animal, Date, Type, Details, Cost.

Tables and boards

  • Fields (Board)
  • Irrigation log (Table)
  • Livestock (Table)
  • Health records (Table)

On Home

Figures: Head of livestock, Planted area, dunam, Water this month, m³.

Alerts when something needs you: Vaccinations due this week, Harvest within two weeks.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Plan the season
  • Vaccination schedule

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Owner: sees every item, and edits
  • Farm worker: sees every item, and edits
  • Vet: sees every item, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Farming and livestock. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
