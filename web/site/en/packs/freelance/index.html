Clients, quotes, invoices with VAT, payments and hours, for people who work for themselves.

What it keeps 6

  • ClientsA person or company you work for: Company, Phone, Status
  • QuotesA price you offer for a piece of work: Client, Amount before VAT, Sent, Valid until, Status
  • InvoicesWhat a client owes you, with VAT: Client, Invoice number, Subtotal, VAT 15%, Total, Issued, Due, Status
  • PaymentsMoney received from a client: Client, Invoice, Amount, Date, Method
  • HoursTime spent on work for a client: Client, Date, Hours, Hourly rate, Billed
  • ContractsAn agreement with a client and when it ends: Client, Starts, Ends, Value, Signed
