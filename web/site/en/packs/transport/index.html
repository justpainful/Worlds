Packs / Field work

The Transport and delivery pack

Trips, drivers, vehicles with their servicing and papers, and shipments by status.

What the pack adds

  • Shipments from booked to delivered
  • Trips with their driver and vehicle
  • Vehicles with service, registration and insurance dates
  • Drivers and their licence expiry

What you keep in it

Shipments

Goods going from one place to another. Fields: Tracking number, From, To, Trip, Deliver by, Status.

Trips

A vehicle's journey with its driver. Fields: Driver, Vehicle, Date, Route, Distance, km, Fuel cost.

Vehicles

A truck, van or car and its papers. Fields: Plate, Odometer, Next service, Registration expires, Insurance expires.

Drivers

Someone who drives for you. Fields: Phone, Licence expires, Available.

Tables and boards

  • Shipments (Board)
  • Trips (Table)
  • Vehicles (Table)
  • Drivers (Table)

On Home

Figures: On the way, Kilometres this month, Fuel this month.

Alerts when something needs you: Late shipments, Registrations expiring this month, Insurance expiring this month, Licences expiring this month.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Plan tomorrow's trips
  • Tell a customer about a delay

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Dispatcher: sees every item, and edits
  • Driver: sees only what is assigned to them, and edits
  • Customer: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Transport and delivery. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
