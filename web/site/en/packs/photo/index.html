Packs / Creative, tech and media

The Photo and video pack

Shoots, clients, deliveries and gear, for photographers and video makers.

What the pack adds

  • Shoots on the calendar with their client and place
  • Deliveries with a deadline
  • Gear with its value and service
  • Payments due from each shoot

What you keep in it

Shoots

A session you photograph or film. Fields: Client, Type, Date, Time, Place, Price, Deposit paid, Status, Deliver by.

Clients

Who you shoot for. Fields: Phone, Instagram.

Gear

A camera, lens, light or drone. Fields: Serial number, Value, Next service, Insured.

Tables and boards

  • Shoots (Board)
  • Deliveries (Table)
  • Clients (Table)
  • Gear (Table)

On Home

Figures: Shoots this month, Booked this month, Gear value.

Alerts when something needs you: Late deliveries.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write a shoot brief
  • Write a price list

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Photographer: sees every item, and edits
  • Assistant: sees every item, and edits
  • Client: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Photo and video. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
