Packs / Education and training

The Training centres pack

Courses, trainees, sessions and certificates, for training centres and trainers.

What the pack adds

  • Courses with their trainer, dates and fees
  • Trainees and what they have paid
  • Sessions on the calendar with attendance
  • Certificates issued and still to issue

What you keep in it

Courses

A course you run, its dates and seats. Fields: Trainer, Starts, Ends, Seats, Fee, Status.

Trainees

Someone taking a course. Fields: Course, Phone, Amount paid, Certificate issued.

Sessions

One meeting of a course. Fields: Course, Date, Starts, Attended, Topic.

Tables and boards

  • Courses (Board)
  • Trainees (Table)
  • Sessions (Table)
  • Certificates to issue (Table)

On Home

Figures: Running courses, Trainees, Fees received.

Alerts when something needs you: Courses starting this week.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Outline a course
  • Write a course announcement

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Centre manager: sees every item, and edits
  • Trainer: sees only what is assigned to them, and edits
  • Trainee: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Training centres. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
