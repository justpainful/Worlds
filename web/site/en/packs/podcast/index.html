Packs / Creative, tech and media

The Podcast and media pack

Episodes from idea to published, guests, outlines and scripts, and the publishing schedule.

What the pack adds

  • Episodes on a board from idea to published
  • Guests and when they are booked
  • Outlines and scripts with each episode
  • A publishing schedule

What you keep in it

Episodes

One episode and where it is. Fields: Number, Guest, Recording, Publish on, Status, Length, minutes, Listens.

Guests

Someone you interview. Fields: Who they are, Phone, Social account, Status.

Tables and boards

  • Episodes (Board)
  • Publishing schedule (Table)
  • Guests (Board)
  • Listens (Table)

On Home

Figures: Published, Total listens, Guests confirmed.

Alerts when something needs you: Publishing this week and not edited.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Outline an episode
  • Write episode notes

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Host: sees every item, and edits
  • Producer: sees every item, and edits
  • Audio editor: sees only what is assigned to them, and edits

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Podcast and media. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
