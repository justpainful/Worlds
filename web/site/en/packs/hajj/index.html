Packs / Services and hospitality

The Hajj, Umrah and travel companies pack

Groups, travellers and their documents, housing, buses and the programme day by day.

What the pack adds

  • Groups with their dates and leader
  • Travellers with passport and visa status
  • Housing and bus seats for each group
  • A day by day programme
  • Warnings for passports about to expire

What you keep in it

Groups

Travellers going together. Fields: Group leader, Departs, Returns, Travellers, Status.

Travellers

One person and their papers. Fields: Travel group, Phone, Passport expires, Visa, Vaccinated, Amount paid.

Housing

Where a group stays. Fields: Travel group, Hotel, City, From, To, Rooms.

Buses

A bus and its driver for a group. Fields: Travel group, Driver, Driver's phone, Seats.

Programme

What a group does on a day. Fields: Travel group, Date, Place.

Tables and boards

  • Groups (Board)
  • Travellers (Table)
  • Visas (Board)
  • Housing (Table)
  • Buses (Table)
  • Programme (Table)

On Home

Figures: Travellers, Visas issued, Payments received.

Alerts when something needs you: Passports expiring within six months, Visas not issued, Not vaccinated.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Write the group programme
  • Message travellers before departure

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Company manager: sees every item, and edits
  • Group leader: sees only what is assigned to them, and edits
  • Traveller: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Hajj, Umrah and travel companies. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
