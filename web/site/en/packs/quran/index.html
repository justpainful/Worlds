Packs / Education and training

The Quran circles pack

Circles and students, memorisation and revision by surah and ayah, and attendance.

What the pack adds

  • Circles with their teacher and time
  • Each student's memorisation and revision, surah by surah
  • A grade for every recitation
  • Attendance and who has not recited this week

What you keep in it

Circles

A group that meets to memorise together. Fields: Teacher, Day, Starts, Place.

Students

One student and how far they have memorised. Fields: Study circle, Parts memorised, Now memorising, Parent or guardian, Guardian's phone.

Recitations

A portion recited to the teacher. Fields: Student, Date, Type, Surah, From ayah, To ayah, Grade, Note.

Attendance

Whether a student came on a day. Fields: Student, Date, Status.

Tables and boards

  • Students (Table)

On Home

Figures: Students, Recitations this week, Parts memorised.

Alerts when something needs you: Portions to repeat.

Ready requests for Claude

  • Plan a revision schedule
  • Write to a parent about progress

Pick a request from the pack and Claude gets it with the open item; every change it makes can be undone.

Roles in a Team workspace

  • Supervisor: sees every item, and edits
  • Teacher: sees every item, and edits
  • Parent: sees only what is assigned to them, read only

Get started

  1. Install Worlds or open it in the browser

    From the apps page, or open Worlds on the web.

  2. Turn the pack on

    In Settings, Packs, turn on Quran circles. Or pick it when the welcome asks "What do you use Worlds for?".

  3. Try it with examples

    Add the pack's ready examples to see how it works, and delete them whenever you like.
