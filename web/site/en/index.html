Kept on your device, not ours

Everything you write in Worlds is saved in one file on your device:

%APPDATA%\Worlds\worlds.db

It opens and works offline. An account is optional: you only need one to share a page with someone or sync it between your devices.

⋮⋮

Arabic first, English just as well

The whole interface runs right to left in Arabic on Windows, Android and Apple devices. It was built that way, not mirrored after the fact.

  • SearchFinds a word however its alef or taa marbuta was typed, and reads Arabic digits.
  • DatesThe Hijri date sits beside every day in the calendar and on Home, with the Friday and Saturday weekend shaded.
  • Quick addType “Call the bank tomorrow 4pm !!” and it becomes a task due tomorrow at four, marked important.
  • ShortcutsEvery shortcut works on an Arabic keyboard, and digits typed on it count in spreadsheets.
⋮⋮

Claude edits the page itself

Open the Claude panel in Worlds, or connect Claude on your computer through MCP, and ask: “sort this week’s tasks by due date” or “turn these notes into a table”. It reads your pages and changes their blocks directly, instead of handing you text to paste.

Claude edited “Weekly plan”Moved 3 tasks and added a study table
Undo

Every edit goes into the page history, and one click takes it back.

⋮⋮

One page, everyone in their place

Make a workspace for your team or class and invite people by email. Each page has its level: full access, edit, comment or view, and the server enforces it.

Sign in with a six-digit code by email or with a passkey on your device, and see your colleagues’ cursors as they write on the same page.

Download Worlds

Every build is in the latest release on GitHub.

Windows.exe installer. Installs offline. Not signed yet: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
Android and ChromebookOne .apk for every device.
iPhone and iPad.ipa file to sign with a tool such as SideStore.
Mac.dmg. The first time, right-click it and choose Open.
Browsergo.enterworlds.space, nothing to install. Your pages stay in the browser, and on your account when you sign in.
