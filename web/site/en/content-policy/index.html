Content policy

Most of what you make in Worlds stays on your device and is nobody's business but yours. This policy is about what you make public with Worlds: public profiles, published pages and share links.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Not allowed

  • Illegal content, including anything that sexualises children.
  • Harassment and threats against a person or a group, and publishing someone's private information without their permission.
  • Impersonation: pretending to be another person, a company, or Worlds itself.
  • Scams and harmful links: phishing, malware, and pages made to trick people out of money or passwords.
  • Other people's work shared without the right to share it.
  • Hate that attacks people for who they are.
  • Using Worlds as a file host for files meant for people outside your workspace.

Usernames, display names and public profiles may not contain insults or offensive words, in any language.

Reporting

Anyone can report a public profile, published page or share link from the support site, with its link and the reason. The Worlds team reads every report. If several people report the same content, it may be hidden while we look at it.

What happens

  • Content that breaks this policy is removed or hidden, and its owner gets an email that says what and why.
  • Serious or repeated breaches can lead to a suspension or a ban of the account, as described in the terms of use.
  • Pages on the owner's own device are never touched.

Appeals

If you think a decision about your content is wrong, reply to the email you received or send a request from the support site. Someone who did not make the first decision looks at it again.
