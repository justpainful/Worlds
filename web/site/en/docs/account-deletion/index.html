Docs / Account and sharing

Account deletion

You can delete your Worlds account yourself, at any time, from the app or from Worlds on the web. You have 30 days to change your mind. Your pages on your own device are never deleted.

Before you delete

  • Download your data: in Settings, Account, choose Download My Data. You get one file with everything the account service keeps about you.
  • Shared workspaces you own: if other people are in them, hand them over to someone else (Sharing) or delete them first. Worlds asks you to do this before it deletes the account.
  • Your pages on this device stay where they are. Deleting the account does not touch them.

Deleting the account

  1. Open account settings

    In the app: Settings, Account. On the web: app.enterworlds.space, then Settings, Account.

  2. Choose Delete Account

    Worlds shows what happens next and the date your account will be erased.

  3. Confirm it is you

    With your passkey (fingerprint, face or PIN), or with a 6-digit code sent to your email.

At once, every device signs out, and your public profile, published pages, share links and Worlds mail are hidden. You get an email from support@enterworlds.space with the date.

Changing your mind

For 30 days, sign in again and choose Cancel Deletion. Everything comes back as it was, and you get an email saying so.

What is erased after 30 days

  • Your account, passkeys, devices, sessions and workspace memberships.
  • Your public profile and its pictures, your published pages and share links.
  • Your Worlds mail address and anything waiting in it.
  • The web copy of your workspace and its files, and your notifications.
  • Workspaces you owned alone, with their pages, versions and files.

The security log keeps what happened without linking it to you, and support requests stay without your name or email. The details are in the privacy policy.

If you cannot sign in

Send a request from the support site and choose Delete my account. We check that the account is yours with a code to its email, then delete it the same way.
