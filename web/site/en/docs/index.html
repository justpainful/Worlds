Worlds docs

Every part of Worlds explained: installing it on your device, writing, organising, drawing and presenting, connecting Claude and sharing your pages.

First time? Start with Install, then Getting started. If you would rather not install anything, open Worlds in the browser.

Start here

  • OverviewEverything you need to install and use Worlds, from the first launch to connecting Claude and sharing with your team.
  • Getting started with WorldsThe first launch of Worlds, where your workspace is kept, the sample workspace, the sidebar, Home, and language and text direction.

Install

  • Install WorldsWhich file to download for your device, what works on each platform today, the requirements, and how to check the file is intact.
  • Install Worlds on WindowsSteps to install Worlds on Windows 10 and 11, with the SmartScreen window, the setup wizard and what you see the first time it opens.
  • Install Worlds on MacSteps to install Worlds on macOS 14 and later: dragging it to Applications, and opening it the first time from Privacy & Security or with a right click.
  • Install Worlds on iPhone and iPadSteps to install the unsigned ipa file with SideStore, turn on Developer Mode, trust the developer, and refresh every 7 days with a free Apple ID.
  • Install Worlds on AndroidSteps to install the APK on Android 8.0 and later: the permission to install unknown apps, then the install, with Play Protect and Chromebooks.
  • Worlds in the browserUse Worlds at app.enterworlds.space without installing, and learn where your pages are kept in the browser and on your account, and how the demo differs.
  • Update and uninstallHow to update or remove Worlds on Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android and the browser, and where your pages stay after you remove it.
  • TroubleshootingCommon Worlds install and startup problems and their fixes: SmartScreen, WebView2, macOS messages, installing on Android and iPhone, private browsing, and the sign in code.

Write and organise

  • Pages and blocksHow to write in Worlds pages: the "/" menu and every block type, the block handle, moving and nesting, icons and covers, properties, links between pages, pins and favorites.
  • DocumentsThe Worlds document editor: paper pages with a size and margins, paragraph styles and fonts, page setup, and printing or saving as PDF.
  • Tables and spreadsheetsSpreadsheets in Worlds: cell references like A1, the formulas and functions that really exist, shortcuts, CSV and Excel import and export, and tables inside pages.
  • Projects and resourcesThe kinds of content in Worlds, creating a resource from the sidebar, a project that groups related resources, and study items: subjects, lessons, assignments and exams.
  • TemplatesTemplates in Worlds: using a ready-made template, saving your page as a template, editing, duplicating and deleting templates, and the ready-made visual canvases.
  • Search and commandsSearching all your pages, the Ctrl+K command palette, quick capture and quick add that understand "tomorrow 4pm" and «بكرة», and a table of keyboard shortcuts.
  • History and backupsHow Worlds keeps your work: autosave and undo, page history and earlier versions, Trash and restore, and the daily database backup.

Draw, present and media

  • DrawingThe Worlds drawing editor: tools and their shortcuts, brushes, shapes and shape correction, layers, masks, adjustments, the eraser, versions, experiments, export, and linking a drawing to a resource.
  • PresentationsPresentations in Worlds: slides and layouts, text, pictures and shapes, slide backgrounds, speaker notes, and presenting full screen with its keys.
  • Media and filesThe Worlds media gallery, file preview and the file types it shows, stream links, and attachments inside pages, where they are kept and their limits.

Claude and automations

  • Claude and other AI providersHow to connect Claude, Codex, an API key or a local model to Worlds, what the assistant can do, how to undo its changes and where keys are kept.
  • AutomationsSend a page to Discord at a set time or summarise it with AI: creating an automation, schedules, the run history, approval and notifications.
  • Discord bridgeHow Worlds sends a page to a Discord channel, thread or DM through your own bot, the preview before sending, and the approval queue.
  • IntegrationsThe Integrations view in Worlds on Windows: the Claude Code card and the Discord card, and what each one really does.

Account and sharing

  • Your Worlds accountWhy you might want a Worlds account, how to create one, signing in with an emailed 6-digit code, passkeys and signing out.
  • Sharing and working togetherSharing a page in Worlds: Team workspaces, invite links, roles and permissions enforced by the server, seeing who is here, and offline editing.
  • How sync worksA plain explanation of what stays on your device and what goes to the Worlds server, how the web version keeps its workspace on your account, and how conflicts are settled.
  • PacksPacks in Worlds: what a pack is, how to turn on the Study pack, what it adds (subjects, a timetable, assignments and exams), and the other packs being built.
  • Profile and ActivityThe Worlds profile page: name, avatar, banner, status, blocks and stats, and the Activity view that shows what changed recently and what waits for your approval.
  • SettingsEvery Settings section in Worlds on Windows, in order and with the names the app shows, and what each one holds.

For developers

  • HTML pagesHTML pages in Worlds: a page whose content is one HTML document running in a sandbox, such as a calculator, a tracker or a report. The rules, worlds.save, how they look on each platform and how Claude writes them.
  • The Worlds MCP serverConnect Claude Code or any MCP client to the Worlds workspace on your computer: the setup command, the tool groups, a version saved before every write with undo, and Discord sends that wait for your approval.
