Install Worlds on iPhone and iPad

Worlds is not in the App Store yet, and its file is unsigned. So a tool such as SideStore signs it with your own Apple ID and installs it. You need a computer only once, to set up the tool.

Before you start

iPhone file
Worlds-0.3.2-iPhone-Unsigned.ipa (12.1 MB)
iPad file
Worlds-0.3.2-iPad-Unsigned.ipa (12.1 MB)
System
iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later
You also need
An Apple ID (a free one is enough), a signing tool such as SideStore or AltStore, and a Windows PC or a Mac to set it up the first time
Signature lasts
7 days with a free Apple ID, then you refresh it from the tool. A full year with a paid Apple Developer membership.

Download the iPhone file for an iPhone and the iPad file for an iPad. The iPad file does not install on an iPhone. The iPhone and iPad app is an early version: see what works on each platform.

Why a signing tool

An iPhone or iPad runs only signed apps. Apple signs App Store apps. Outside the store, anyone with an Apple ID can sign an app for their own devices only. That is what SideStore does: it signs the file with your account, installs it on your device, and renews the signature before it runs out. The Worlds file is not sent anywhere else.

The steps here are written for SideStore. In AltStore they are nearly the same: the My Apps tab, then the + button.

Install step by step

  1. Install SideStore once

    Follow the official SideStore guide. It asks for a computer once, your Apple ID, and a cable to connect the device. Only type your Apple ID password into a tool you installed yourself from its official site. If SideStore or AltStore is already on your device, go to the next step.

  2. Turn on Developer Mode

    iOS needs Developer Mode to run apps signed this way. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll to Developer Mode under Security.

    Turn the switch on and tap Restart. When the device is back, it asks whether to turn on Developer Mode: tap Turn On and enter your passcode. The Developer Mode row appears only after the first app installed this way, such as SideStore itself.

  3. Trust the developer

    The first time you open an app signed with your account, iOS may say the developer is not trusted. Open Settings, then General, then VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, tap your Apple ID email, tap the Trust button, then confirm.

    You do this once per account, and it covers every app you sign with it.

  4. Download the ipa file on the device itself

    Open the latest release page in Safari on the iPhone or iPad. Under Assets, tap the file for your device, then Download. It is saved in the Files app, in Downloads.

  5. Add the file in SideStore

    Open SideStore, go to the My Apps tab and tap the + button at the top. The Files picker opens: choose the .ipa file in Downloads. SideStore signs and installs it, and may ask you to sign in with your Apple ID the first time. Wait until Worlds appears in the list and its icon is on the Home Screen.

  6. Open Worlds

    Tap the Worlds icon. If an untrusted developer message appears, go back to step 3.

Refreshing every 7 days

With a free Apple ID the signature runs out after 7 days, and the app does not open until you refresh it. The button next to Worlds in My Apps shows the days left: tap it to refresh Worlds, or refresh all apps at once from SideStore. Your pages are not deleted when you refresh, or when the signature runs out, as long as you do not delete the app.

A free account also has a limit: 3 signed apps at a time, and SideStore itself is one of them. Details in Troubleshooting.

What you see the first time

On iPhone there are tabs at the bottom: Home, Spaces, Library, AI and Search. On iPad there is a sidebar instead. Parts of the interface are still in English only.

  • To open a Worlds workspace: from the More menu choose Open Database…, then pick the file worlds.db in the Files app, with its attachments folder.
  • A file in iCloud Drive or another cloud folder opens read only, with a note that says why. To edit, make a local copy of it (Create local copy). The local copy is independent, lives inside the app, and does not sync back to the original.
  • The assistant (AI) chats with a provider you choose and an API key you enter in its settings; the key is kept in the device's Keychain. It does not edit pages yet.

If something goes wrong

  • Untrusted DeveloperTrusting your account in VPN & Device Management.
  • Developer Mode requiredWhere the switch is and why it may be missing.
  • Worlds will not open after a weekThe signature ran out; refresh it in SideStore.
