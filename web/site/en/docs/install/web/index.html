Worlds in the browser

Open app.enterworlds.space and start right away. Nothing to install, no files. Your pages are kept in your browser, and on your account too when you sign in.

In short

Address
https://app.enterworlds.space
Browser
A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer, a phone or a Chromebook
Without an account
Your pages are in this browser on this device only
With an account
Your pages are in the browser and on your account, and you open them on any device where you sign in
Demo
https://demo.enterworlds.space, with a ready made workspace to try, and nothing kept on any account

Getting started

  1. Open the address

    Type app.enterworlds.space in the address bar. Use a normal window, not a private or incognito one, because a private window does not keep your pages after you close it.

  2. Choose how to start

    The Welcome to Worlds window appears. Click Start in This Browser to begin without an account, or Sign In or Create Account to keep your pages on your account too.

  3. If you chose to sign in

    Type your email (and your name if you are creating an account). A 6 digit code arrives from no-reply@enterworlds.space and works for 10 minutes. Type it and click Continue. You stay signed in on this browser on later visits.

    In the browser you sign in with the email code only. Passkeys are available in the Windows app.

Where your pages are kept

  • In the browser: Worlds saves your whole workspace and its attachments in the browser's storage for this site (IndexedDB) after every change. Closing the tab loses nothing.
  • Each browser has its own copy: Chrome and Edge on the same computer each have their own pages, and so do your phone and your computer. To bring them together, sign in.
  • On your account: when you sign in, the browser copy and the account copy are merged, then every save is sent to the account a few seconds later. If another device saved first, the newer copy is fetched and merged.
  • What deletes them: clearing site data or cookies for this site deletes the browser copy. Safari may also delete data of sites you have not opened for some days. The account copy stays.

Without an account, your pages exist in this browser only. Before you clear browser data or change devices, sign in so they are kept on your account.

The demo

At demo.enterworlds.space you find Worlds with a ready made sample workspace to look around in: a student's week. It is for trying out, and keeps nothing on any account. For real work, use app.enterworlds.space or one of the apps.

How it differs from the apps

  • The interface is the same as on Windows, in Arabic or English.
  • The assistant (Claude and the other providers) does not work in the browser. It works in the Windows, Android and Apple apps.

If something goes wrong

  • My pages disappearedA private window, or cleared browser data.
  • The sign in code did not arriveThe spam folder, and the sender no-reply@enterworlds.space.
